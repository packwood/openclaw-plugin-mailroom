# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in the Mailroom community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of contribution.

Contributors should be respectful, constructive, specific, and mindful that
reviewers are protecting software capable of external side effects. Harassment,
personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publication of private
communications or credentials are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security-advisory channel. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
